Our process

Four steps to production.

No 40-page discovery decks. Just fast, visible progress from kickoff to launch.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Audit & Strategy

    We map your workflows, find the highest-leverage automation opportunities, and cost every one of them.

    • Full process audit across your team
    • Costed roadmap ranked by ROI
    • A written plan, not a slide deck
  2. 02

    Step 2: Build & Integrate

    We build against your real systems from week one. No sandboxed demos that never ship.

    • Production code, not prototypes
    • Connects to your existing stack
    • Weekly demos, not silence
  3. 03

    Step 3: Test & Refine

    We stress-test with your team using real data before anything goes live.

    • Real-data testing with your team
    • Edge cases handled before launch
    • Feedback folded in fast
  4. 04

    Step 4: Launch & Enable

    We ship it, train your team to run it, and stay only as long as you need us.

    • Hands-on training sessions
    • Documentation for future hires
    • We leave when you don't need us
